| Aspect | Before | After | Impact |

| **Font** | DM Sans 600 | Plus Jakarta Sans 800 | +33% bolder, friendlier |
| **Framing** | "Agente" | "Copiloto" | Collaborative vs autonomous |
| **H1↔Subheading** | 12px | 24px | +100% breathing room |
| **Subheading↔CTAs** | 24px | 32px | +33% clearer hierarchy |
| **CTAs↔Input** | 32px | 48px | +50% visual separation |
| **CTA Count** | 1 | 2 | More conversion options |
| **Max Width** | 720px | 760px | Better line length |
| **Feel** | Corporate | Corgi-like | Warmer, cleaner |
